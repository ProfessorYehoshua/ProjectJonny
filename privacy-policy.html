Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2025

1. Introduction

Jonathan Papia Holistic Health ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit our website or submit a contact form.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit our contact form, we may collect:

  • Your full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (if provided)
  • Service interest and any message you share

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and schedule a discovery call
  • Send follow-up communications related to your inquiry
  • Send SMS messages if you have opted in to receive them

4. SMS Communication

By checking the SMS consent box on our contact form, you agree to receive text messages from Jonathan Papia Holistic Health regarding your inquiry, appointment scheduling, and related services. Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any SMS message.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except as required to operate our services (e.g., form processing via Netlify) or as required by law.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by applicable law.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. To make a request, contact us at jonathanpapiawellness@gmail.com.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at jonathanpapiawellness@gmail.com.
